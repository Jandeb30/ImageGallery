If you reached this far, listen.

It’s bittersweet to think that it’s over between us but it is what it is. Dar, if you reached this far, please listen to me. I know that it is officially over but that doesn’t mean that what we had will just turn into nothing. I hope that you valued what we had in those 21 months of being together. I am not mad at you nga imo najud gi end. I am mad because it even came across your mind. I am mad that you even think about it. I don’t imagine you will receive this letter but I nonetheless must send it. There’s a good girl within you, Sarah. I miss you, and I will always miss you but I cannot live like that, and it seems you cannot live any other way. Dar, if one day you find someone better than me, someone who can give you the thing I never could, please choose your happiness. I wish I knew how to show up better, love better, and be better for the people you matter to me. You’re the only person I talk to, so it hurts that even you will be gone in my life. I love you, Sarah. Nothing hurts more than having a person who makes you feel so special one day, then makes you feel so unwanted the next day. Dar, I hope you knew how hurt I am seeing those words. Kay murag from the start, wala najud ka ganahi nako or wala jud ganahi. Samot pajud nga imong gi share sa uban. You disrespected my privacy. Miskan dili nimo intention nga pakauwawan ko but sa imong gi buhat, gipakauwawan nako nimo. That’s why I don’t want to go out anymore. Yes, I did something wrong that is not acceptable, but miskan na, dili na nimo ipang tabi sa uban kay makaguba ka ug image sa usa ka taw. I hope you understand. You are really brave, Sarah, but also very weak.
